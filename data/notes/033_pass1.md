Here are integration research notes for: LinkedIn Ads (LinkedIn Marketing APIs)

- primary_auth: OAuth2
- auth_methods: OAuth2 (3‑legged member authorization; 2‑legged client credentials not available for Marketing APIs)
- access: app_review (apply in the Developer Portal for each Marketing/Advertising product; LinkedIn reviews and approves access and tier upgrades)
- api_breadth: broad (campaign management, reporting/analytics, conversions, lead sync, matched audiences, community/page, events, etc.)
- mcp: none known
- buildability: ready_with_caveats
- blocker: app_review

Product summary
- One-line: Programmatic access to LinkedIn’s Marketing platform to create/manage ad campaigns, audiences, leads, and reporting across LinkedIn Ads.

Docs URL
- https://learn.microsoft.com/linkedin/marketing

Other implementation details
- OpenAPI spec: None published; Postman workspace is referenced in docs.
- Webhooks: Yes (Lead Sync “leadNotifications” subscriptions; webhook validation required).
- Sandbox: No separate sandbox; all calls are on production data. Dev tier limits apply; you can create one “test” ad account via API in Development tier.

Key notes and constraints for an agent toolkit
- Auth: Use 3‑legged OAuth2 to act on a customer’s ad accounts; 2‑legged flow is not available for Marketing APIs. ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/authentication?utm_source=openai))
- Access gating: You must apply for the Advertising/Marketing APIs in the Developer Portal; all apps start at Development tier, and upgrades (e.g., Standard) require review and evidence of a working integration. Some APIs (e.g., Matched Audiences, Audience Insights) have additional approvals. ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/quick-start?view=li-lms-2026-10&utm_source=openai))
- Scope model: Fine‑grained scopes such as r_ads, rw_ads, rw_conversions, r_marketing_leadgen_automation; member must have appropriate ad account roles. ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/integrations/ads-reporting/conversions-api?view=li-lms-2026-09&utm_source=openai))
- Base path and versioning: Versioned REST under https://api.linkedin.com/rest/. ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/tips-to-get-started?view=li-lms-2026-05&utm_source=openai))
- Lead delivery: Real‑time lead push supported via validated webhooks (Lead Sync). ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/lead-sync/leadsync?view=li-lms-2026-10&utm_source=openai))
- Environment: No sandbox; calls operate on production data under tier limits. ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/integrations/marketing-tiers?view=li-lms-2026-04&viewFallbackFrom=li-lms-2025-02))

Why buildability = ready_with_caveats
- The APIs are well‑documented and stable, but any shipped toolkit will need an approved LinkedIn app (per‑product), scopes, and adherence to tier limits; several high‑value APIs require additional approval. No sandbox and strict review processes mean integration is feasible but not instantly self‑serve. ([learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/quick-start?view=li-lms-2026-10&utm_source=openai))

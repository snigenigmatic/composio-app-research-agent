Notes and nuances for an agent toolkit
- Keys are per-user and limited to meetings recorded by or shared with that user/team; there are admin view controls but no org‑wide key. Prefer OAuth2 for multi‑tenant installs. ([help.fathom.video](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/8368641))
- OAuth apps have a couple data‑shape restrictions on list endpoints (must fetch transcript/summary per recording). ([developers.fathom.ai](https://developers.fathom.ai/quickstart))
- Webhooks carry an HMAC‑verifiable signature; use their verify helpers or reproduce the HMAC‑SHA256 check. ([developers.fathom.ai](https://developers.fathom.ai/webhooks))
- Rate limit is 60 rpm per user across all keys; design for webhooks-first to avoid polling. ([help.fathom.video](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/8368641))
